# Search and Quick Open

## Search your notes

Type in the sidebar's **Search Notes** field. The **Search** card lists the notes that match, with a snippet and
where each note is.

- Every word you type must be in the note's name or text.
- Search covers the notes folder, connected GitHub repositories, open projects and files you opened on their own.
- Names that match come first.
- With no match, the card says **No results**.

Click or tap a result to open it. Clear the field to see your places again.

## Quick Open

Quick Open finds a note by its name or by what it says, and runs commands.

- **On the Mac**, choose **File ▸ Quick Open…** or press ⌘K.
- **On iPad**, press ⌘K on a keyboard, or use **Quick Open…** on the welcome.
- **On iPhone**, use **Quick Open…** on the welcome.

Type in **Search notes and commands**:

- With nothing typed, it shows your **Recent** notes and the **Commands**.
- **Notes** lists notes whose name matches.
- **Inside Notes** lists notes whose text matches, with a snippet.

Use ↑ and ↓ to move, and Return to open. Press Esc, or click outside, to close it. Opening a note shows it in the
sidebar too.

### Commands

- **New Note “…”**: makes a note named after what you typed. With nothing typed, it's **New Note**.
- **New Folder**
- **Welcome to Markdown**: shows the welcome.

On the Mac, Quick Open shows each command's shortcut.
